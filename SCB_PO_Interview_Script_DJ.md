**Number one, Commercial Viability:** *(停顿)* Are we driving the Total Payment Volume and generating new fee-based revenue? I always set aggressive commercial targets here—just like when we achieved that 100x volume growth in our lending product.

## 7. Product Deep-Dives

### 7.1 PayAll

To put it simply, the core essence of Citi PayAll was **'unlocking the payment boundaries of credit cards.'**

**First, let's look at the Problem:** Traditionally, credit cards only work if a merchant accepts them and pays the processing fee (MDR). This creates a 'boundary'—you typically can't use a card for **high-ticket, essential expenses** like rent, income tax, or school fees.

**The Solution was to build a 'Card-to-Cash' engine:** PayAll breaks this wall by allowing a frictionless bank transfer to the payee. The landlord or the tax bureau receives cash directly into their bank account, while the user uses their credit limit to pay, earning valuable air miles and enjoying an interest-free float for a small service fee.

**From a PO perspective, the value is transformative:**
We shifted the credit card battlefield from everyday 'dining and shopping' to capturing **massive household cash flows**. This achieved three things:
1. It created a significant new **fee-based revenue** stream for the bank.
2. It dramatically increased **customer stickiness** (because users won't switch banks if their rent and taxes are automated).
3. And it allowed us to tap into the 'un-merchanted' payment space, which is a huge blue ocean.

---

### 7.2 EPP/LOP (Installment Feature)

For products like EPP and LOP, my strategy was to transform them from static financial products into an **Embedded Smart Financial Hub**.

The core logic is a paradigm shift: **moving from 'Users seeking services' to 'Services finding users.'**

Traditionally, a user has to open an App, find the installment menu, and apply. That's too much friction. What we built is a **trigger-based engine**:
When a large transaction happens, our backend instantly pre-screens the user. If they qualify, we send a secure, **App-less SMS link** at that exact 'moment of need.'

This approach does three things:
1. **It eliminates friction:** The user converts in seconds without downloading or registering.
2. **It reactivates dormant customers:** We reach users who don't use our App but have high-value spending potential.
3. **It drives hyper-efficiency:** This is exactly how we achieved that **100x volume growth**—by being 'invisible in the background, but visible when it matters most.'

To me, this is the future of retail banking: **Contextual Lending** that feels like a service, not a sales pitch.

### 7.3 Digital Insurance

**Product Vision: The Embedded Intelligent Insurance Advisor**

Based on the operational model of **"Data Screening → SMS Outreach → Direct Link Purchase,"** this feature aims to act as an embedded advisor offering **"Precise Insight and Instant Protection."** It shifts the core logic of insurance from "passive selling" to "proactive safeguarding."

**Core Positioning: Service Seeking the User**

Unlike traditional "shelf-style" insurance that relies on pushy sales calls, this feature transforms insurance into a scenario-based, instant service. By analyzing consumption behaviors, it identifies protection gaps and delivers the right coverage exactly when the user needs it most.

**Key Pillars: Building a Trust-Driven Conversion Chain**

- **Data-Driven Precision (Smart Screening):** Acts as a "risk-aware brain." Instead of mass messaging, it analyzes transaction profiles (e.g., booking flights, buying gas, medical expenses) to push tailor-made products only to users in immediate risk scenarios.
- **Frictionless Purchase (Trust Loop):** Simplifies complex insurance processes into a minimalist "click, understand, and buy" experience. Backed by the bank's credibility, it removes resistance and builds immediate trust.
- **Scenario-Based Anchoring:** Embeds insurance directly into life events. (e.g., Pushing flight accident insurance right after an airline ticket purchase). It delivers the exact peace of mind needed at that specific moment.

**Value Proposition: A Win-Win Ecosystem**

- **For Users (Seamless Peace of Mind):** No harassing calls or complex terms. Users receive timely, easy-to-activate protection for themselves and their families in the most natural way.
- **For Banks (Stickiness & Revenue):** Expands the bank's role from "payment tool" to "life protection," boosting customer loyalty and driving intermediate business revenue with low friction.
- **Long-Term Value:** Gradually cultivates users' risk management habits, turning them from passive receivers to proactive planners.

**Future Outlook**

The system will evolve toward **"Dynamic Coverage & Pay-on-Demand."** For example, detecting high-frequency travel to automatically offer a highly affordable, 7-day short-term driving insurance—existing only when needed without adding long-term financial burdens.

**In One Sentence:**
It is an intelligent insurance advisor that *"hides within consumption scenarios and appears at risk gaps"*—using the shortest path and lightest touch to transform insurance from a complex sales pitch into simple, timely protection exactly when the user needs it most.
